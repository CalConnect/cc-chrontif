== Scheme-maintained registries

=== General

A scheme implementing this document (a "scheme") shall maintain registries for
the extensible value spaces used throughout the framework. This document does
not prescribe the content of any registry. Each scheme owns and maintains its
own registries, defining the specific values recognized within that scheme's
scope of recognition.

This annex specifies which registries a scheme shall maintain, the
characteristics each registry shall have, and the registration policy
requirements that apply.


=== Required registries

A scheme shall maintain at minimum the following registries:

.Required scheme-maintained registries
[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Registry | Purpose | Referenced by

| Trust dimension tag registry
| Identifies trust dimension types used in co-signature blocks and verification
  results
| <<artifact-multi-dimensional>>

| Algorithm identifier registry
| Identifies signature algorithms recognized by the scheme, with their class
  (classical, post-quantum, composite), parameter sets, and status
| <<algorithms>>

| Ceremony type registry
| Identifies ceremony types used in ceremony transcripts and deployment
  manifests
| <<ceremony-records>>

| Format profile registry
| Identifies signature envelope format profiles recognized by the scheme, with
  their canonicalization characteristics
| <<artifact-format-profiles>>

| Scope dimension registry
| Identifies scope dimension names used in scope encodings within the scheme
| <<scope-structure>>
|===

A scheme may maintain additional registries as needed for its domain.


=== Registry characteristics

Each registry maintained by a scheme shall have:

* **Name.** A human-readable name identifying the registry.
* **Description.** A statement of the registry's purpose and scope.
* **Entry schema.** The fields that each entry shall contain, including any
  required fields and optional fields.
* **Registration policy.** The criteria and process for adding, modifying, or
  retiring entries.
* **Maintainer.** The organization or body responsible for maintaining the
  registry and performing registration decisions.
* **Publication.** A publicly accessible location where the current registry
  contents are published.


=== Registration policy requirements

Each registry's registration policy shall ensure that:

* registrations are technically sound;
* proposed entries do not duplicate existing entries;
* specifications are sufficiently detailed for independent implementation;
* security implications have been assessed;
* registrations, modifications, and retirements are publicly recorded with
  dates and rationale.

The registration policy may designate expert reviewers. The scheme shall
document the reviewer qualification criteria.


=== Inter-scheme recognition

Two schemes that wish to interoperate shall publish a mapping between their
respective registry entries, or shall adopt a common registry. The mechanism
for inter-scheme recognition is out of scope of this document; it is a
governance decision of the participating schemes.

NOTE: A scheme may adopt another scheme's registry by reference, or may
establish a joint registry with other schemes. The framework does not mandate
a specific inter-scheme recognition mechanism.


=== No mandatory initial values

This document does not prescribe mandatory initial values for any registry.
The initial content of each registry is determined by the scheme at
establishment time, subject to the scheme's own governance process. Profiles
of this document (see <<annex-d>>) may recommend initial values as examples,
but such recommendations are informative.
